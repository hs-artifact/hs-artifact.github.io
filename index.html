FieldTrust, a data trust platform for oil & gas and mining

One product that shows the whole job: engineering the data, proving its quality, governing it, and getting it adopted on site.

Every number on the dashboard carries a trust score

  • Medallion pipeline driven by data contracts. Rows that break a blocking rule are quarantined with the rule ID.
  • Trust score per dataset, from pass rate and reconciliation, shown next to every KPI.
  • Hand-off checks with a money value: well to export meter in oil & gas, pit to hauling in mining.
  • A 15-stage value chain mapped to its data: format, frequency, owner and consumer.
  • Tests run in CI, and the site only publishes when they pass.

At a large coal operation in Kalimantan, I calibrated WIM tonnage against independent references until it met the accuracy class, across 2,500 to 3,500 weighings a day. Adoption rose once crews trusted the number.
