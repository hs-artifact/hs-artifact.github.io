Flagship work

FieldTrust: a data trust platform for oil & gas and mining

One product that shows the whole job: engineering the data, proving its quality, governing it, and getting it adopted on site.

From field event to decision, with a trust score on every number

  • Medallion pipeline driven by data contracts; rows that break a blocking rule are quarantined with the rule ID.
  • Trust score per dataset (pass rate and reconciliation), shown next to every KPI.
  • Live hand-off checks with money value: well → export meter in oil & gas, pit → hauling in mining.
  • Value chain of 15 stages mapped to their data: format, frequency, owner, consumer.
  • Tests run in CI; the site only publishes when they pass.
